The 12 weeks

Five phases, one project, start to finish.

You follow a realistic case, such as a fintech onboarding flow, from kickoff brief to UAT plan, the same way a BA would on the job.

Questions

Before you sign up.

Do I need a tech or IT background?

No. Most career changers come from research, banking, operations, or customer-facing roles. Week 1 starts from the basics.

What if I miss a live session?

Every session is recorded, so you can catch up and still submit that week's assignment.

Will this get me certified?

The program covers the BABOK foundations and ends with a clear ECBA roadmap. Certification exams are taken separately through IIBA.

Is a job guaranteed?

No honest program can promise that. You will leave with a portfolio, a sharper CV, and interview practice, which is what hiring managers look for.

When does it start?

The first cohort begins in November 2026. Exact dates and the session timetable are sent to enrolled students by email and WhatsApp once the cohort is full.

How do I pay?

By bank transfer. After you complete the enrolment form, your account details and a personal reference appear on screen. Use that reference as the transfer narration so your payment can be matched to you.

When is my place confirmed?

Once the transfer lands and is verified, usually within 24 hours. You will get a WhatsApp or email confirmation. Until then your place is reserved, not confirmed.

What is the refund policy?

If you withdraw before the cohort begins, you can request a refund less a 10% administrative fee, or transfer your place once to the next cohort at no cost. Once training has commenced, fees are non-refundable and non-transferable. If BA Bridge postpones or cancels the cohort, you may choose a full refund with no deduction or a place in the next cohort. Refund requests must be sent in writing to ubonanalyst@gmail.com and are processed within 14 working days.
