How it runs

Built to fit around a full-time job.

Live sessions
Two a week: a 2-hour weekday evening class and a 2-hour Saturday workshop. Every session is recorded.
Assignments
One a week, each adding to your capstone, with written feedback on every submission.
Peer pods
Small review groups inside a cohort of 10 to 20, so you learn from how others approach the same problem.
Format
Fully online and live, so you can join from anywhere.

Questions

Before you sign up.

Do I need a tech or IT background?

No. Most career changers come from research, banking, operations, or customer-facing roles. Week 1 starts from the basics.

What if I miss a live session?

Every session is recorded, so you can catch up and still submit that week's assignment.

Will this get me certified?

The program covers the BABOK foundations and ends with a clear ECBA roadmap. Certification exams are taken separately through IIBA.

Is a job guaranteed?

No honest program can promise that. You will leave with a portfolio, a sharper CV, and interview practice, which is what hiring managers look for.

What is the pilot cohort?

A small first group at a reduced fee, in exchange for detailed feedback and a testimonial. Waitlist members hear about it first.

When does it start?

Dates are shared with the waitlist first. Joining now keeps your place in line.
