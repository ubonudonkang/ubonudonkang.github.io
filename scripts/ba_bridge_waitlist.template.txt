scripts/close_ba_registration.mjs to apply it to the BA Training page.
The command requires the closed cohort and next cohort as Month YYYY values.
It disables new checkout on the page while preserving earlier payment returns.
All behaviour lives in js/forms.js; this section only carries data attributes.
Update public copy, pricing, CTAs and structured data before publishing.
