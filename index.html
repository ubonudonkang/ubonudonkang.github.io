Between the problem and the fix.

I'm a Business Analyst based in Lagos, Nigeria, currently working at MTN Nigeria. Before that I spent two and a half years at Access Bank on software delivery, ProcessMaker and RPA projects, including automation rolled out across its African subsidiaries. I've also taken a government agency's accounting system from requirements to launch.

I sit between the people who know the problem and the people who build the fix, and I write things down until nobody has to guess.

Outside the day job, I teach and mentor career changers through the BA Bridge Cohort and write field notes on BRDs, RPA and certification.
